<h2 id="hybrid-and-virtual-only-participation">Hybrid and virtual-only participation</h2>

<p>NAACL 2027 is a hybrid conference, and ReAL-Health is designed to run fully virtually without loss of value.</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Pre-recorded videos.</strong> Every accepted paper supplies a pre-recorded video, released before the workshop, so discussion is not gated on live attendance.</li>
  <li><strong>Dedicated remote moderator.</strong> Talks and the panel are live-streamed, with one organizer whose only role is relaying questions from the chat into the room, so remote attendees are not competing with in-room microphones.</li>
  <li><strong>Two-time-band posters.</strong> Posters run on a virtual platform with synchronous author slots duplicated across an Americas-friendly and a Europe/Asia-friendly band, so no region is confined to asynchronous participation.</li>
  <li><strong>Questions collected in advance.</strong> Panel and open-problems questions are gathered through a public form, equalizing remote and in-person input.</li>
  <li><strong>Persistent channel.</strong> A discussion channel opens one week before the workshop and stays open two weeks after.</li>
  <li><strong>Virtual-only contingency.</strong> If the venue runs virtual-only, the program splits into two shorter half-days in complementary time zones rather than one long day. The shared task is unaffected.</li>
</ul>

<p><small>This schedule is preliminary and subject to change. Times are relative to workshop start; absolute clock times will be posted once NAACL finalizes the workshop day and slot assignment.</small></p>
