Workshop notification is expected on October 2, 2026. If ReAL-Health 2027 is accepted, the first call for papers goes out on October 26, 2026, and the OpenReview submission site will go live here alongside it. The paper submission deadline for NAACL 2027 workshops is February 5, 2027.

The workshop brings together researchers and practitioners working on reliable, auditable, private, and safe clinical language technology, including the safety of increasingly agentic systems operating over the electronic health record.
